\subsection{Implications for Benchmark Temporal Comparability}
\label{sec:disc_temporal}

\looseness=-1
Temporal DIF decomposes benchmark differences into three layers: (1)~\emph{impact}---ability differences removed by matching ($\sim$2.4~pp); (2)~\emph{DIF}---item-level differences after ability control ($\sim$28--29\% floor, 92.4\% item-specific); (3)~\emph{attribution}---contamination, capability evolution, or methodology shifts, underdetermined without training data. Regardless of attribution, temporal DIF means the same score carries different meaning across model generations.

\subsection{Ground Truth Meta-Critique}
\label{sec:disc_groundtruth}

\looseness=-1
The near-independence between DIF flags and web-overlap labels (\Cref{sec:groundtruth}) reflects a category error: web-overlap measures global exposure, while DIF measures differential functioning, making overlap-based labels an inappropriate ground truth for a differential method. Semi-synthetic dose-response confirms detection works when present; the disconnect is in the ground truth.

\subsection{Recommended DIF Audit Protocol}
\label{sec:disc_protocol}

\looseness=-1
We propose running temporal MH-DIF whenever $\geq 500$ new models accumulate ($N \geq 80$ suffices per \Cref{sec:validation}; 500 ensures family diversity). Empirical $\rho$ degradation simulation---DIF-ordered versus random item removal across six benchmarks---grounds graduated thresholds:
(1)~\emph{Monitor} (C\% $< 15\%$): $\Delta\rho < 0.001$ vs.\ random on all benchmarks; report C\% in release notes.
(2)~\emph{Flag} ($15$--$25\%$): $\Delta\rho = 0.001$--$0.003$ on 4/6 benchmarks; report stable-item rankings alongside full rankings.
(3)~\emph{Act} (C\% $\geq 25\%$): WinoGrande drops below $\rho = 0.995$ at 25\% DIF-ordered removal; review flagged item content, cross-reference with contamination indicators, report stable-item rankings as primary comparability metric.
Thresholds reflect where DIF-ordered degradation reliably exceeds random removal. DIF pre-screening identifies stable anchor items for equating~\citep{kolen2014equating,habba2026growingpains}; temporal C\% complements composite quality indices~\citep{zhu2026bhi}.
